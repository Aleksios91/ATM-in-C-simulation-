THIS PROGRAM WILL NOT STEAL YOU ANY MONEY NOR WILL MANIPULATE WITH YOUR BANK ACCOUNT IN ANY WAY/SHAPE/OR FORM AS IT CANNOT ACCESS ANYONE'S BANK ACCOUNT FOR THIS IS JUST A SIMULATION ONLY!! I DON'T CONDONE ANY UNAUTHORIZED MAKE OF ANY ATM PROGRAM THAT IS MADE TO BE USED FOR MALICIOUS PURPOSES!!!!

It presents as detailed simulation of a real ATM interaction as it gets.

Features:
1) Inputting a credit card like number as a starter
2) Simulation of Deposit of money
3) Simulation of Withdrawal of money
4) As well as the ability to check on the history of simulated monetary transactions one preformed in this program
